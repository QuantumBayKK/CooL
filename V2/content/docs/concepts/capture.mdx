The SDK's capture call is an array push behind an async queue: measured p99 well under a millisecond on the caller's thread. It never awaits, never throws into your code, and never grows without bound — past its limit it drops the oldest events and counts them.

If the evidence plane is unreachable, your request still succeeds. The loss shows up in `cool stats` and in your metrics, never in your latency. Fail-open toward the application, fail-closed toward the network: nothing is transmitted to an endpoint that has not attested.
