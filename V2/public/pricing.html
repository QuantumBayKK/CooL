Pricing

Start free.
Pay only to seal.

Verifying is always free. CooL prices like an API: a real free tier, simple plans, and usage-based pricing per sealed change. Anyone can verify any record offline, forever, at no cost and with no account — charging for that would break the one promise the product is built on.

The meter

One unit: a sealed change.

A sealed change is one change to an AI system — a prompt edit, a model swap, a policy change, a granted tool — canonicalised, hashed, signed and written to the log. One record, one unit. You are billed only for changes that successfully seal.
