The meter

One unit: a sealed change.

A sealed change is one change to an AI system — a prompt edit, a model swap, a policy change, a granted tool — canonicalised, hashed, signed and written to the log. One record, one unit. You are billed only for changes that successfully seal.

Because the whole promise is "don't trust us — verify it yourself." Charging for that would put a toll booth in front of the one thing that makes the product credible.
