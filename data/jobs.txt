Steve Jobs believed that customers do not know what they want until you show it to them.

He emphasized that focus means saying no to a thousand things.

Jobs insisted that Apple products start with the user experience and work backward to the technology.

He believed profit was a result of building great products, not the primary goal.

Jobs was known for pushing teams aggressively and demanding perfection.

He famously said that innovation distinguishes between a leader and a follower.
